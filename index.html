I'm new as a firm, but not new to precision work — and with me, you always get the founder, never a junior.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

The things people usually ask before the first call

My books are a total mess. Is that a problem?

That's exactly what the Clean Up service is for — miscategorized transactions, duplicates, unreconciled accounts, all of it. I've spent a decade finding what doesn't belong. Bring me the mess; I'll bring the order.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Burlingame, CA and work with clients locally and fully remote. Everything happens over secure screen-share and email — you never need to leave your office.

How much will it cost?

My pricing is right here on this page: Catch Up is $250 per month of backlog with no minimum, Clean Up is one of three fixed tiers — Light $800 (1–2 accounts), Full $1,500 (3–5 accounts), Deep $2,500 (6+ accounts) — set by a free books review, and Keep Current is $400 (up to 100 transactions), $550 (up to 300), or $700+ (301+) per month based on transaction volume. The first consultation is free, and you'll know the exact price before anything starts.

What software do you use?

QuickBooks — I hold the Intuit Bookkeeping Certification and QuickBooks Certifications (Levels 1 & 2). If you're on something else, we'll talk through it on the free call.

Do you do payroll or file taxes?

I don't run payroll or file tax returns — I keep your books clean, accurate, and tax-ready so your CPA's job is easy. If you need a CPA, I can point you toward good ones.

Is my financial information safe with you?

Yes. After ten years safeguarding patient information under HIPAA, confidentiality is a habit, not a policy. Your books stay between us.
